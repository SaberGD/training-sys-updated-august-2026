## Recording notes
- Record each line as a separate file (`01.wav` … `15.wav`), or all of them in one file with a 1-second gap between lines. I'll split them and place each line in its time slot.
- 48kHz or 44.1kHz, no music, no reverb.
- In the mix, the music ducks automatically under the voice.
